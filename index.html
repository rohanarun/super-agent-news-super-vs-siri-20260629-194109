Super

Super is designed as a personal AI agent that can operate full computers — clicking, typing, navigating — and reuse prior execution through a computer-use cache. That makes Super especially compelling for repeated, multi-step workflows.

Computer use, end to end

Unlike Siri’s app- and OS-level actions, Super focuses on full computer use. This approach mirrors broader industry movement, such as Google Gemini adding computer-use capabilities for AI agents.

Where Super fits in the market

Siri is best for Apple users who want a deeply integrated assistant. Super is for people who want an agent that works across arbitrary websites and apps. Other players like ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Folk, and Orchids help define the space — but Super differentiates itself around real computer use and caching.
